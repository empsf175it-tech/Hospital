Our History & Vision

28 Years of Transforming Healthcare

Vitalis Medical Center opened its doors in 1998 with a singular mission: to deliver tertiary medical care where no clinical compromise is made. Today, we serve over 65,000 patients annually across 24 specialized medical departments.

1998

Foundation & Initial Institute

Established as a specialized 120-bed surgical and cardiac recovery center.

2010

JCI Accreditation & Research Expansion

Awarded Gold Seal of Approval for international patient safety protocols and clinical outcomes.

2018

Robotic Surgery & Oncology Center

Inaugurated state-of-the-art da Vinci Xi surgical suites and comprehensive molecular cancer wing.

2026

Global Medical Excellence Award

Ranked among the premier private tertiary healthcare institutions in the region.
